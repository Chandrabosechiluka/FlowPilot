# T-1030: Brake inspection shows no free slots all week

## Symptoms
Advisor cannot book any Brake Inspection for the whole week; other services are fine.

## Investigation
`get_shop_catalog` showed no technician with the `Brakes` skill. An Admin had re-created technician Arjun's record on Monday to fix a typo in his name and forgot to add the Brakes skill.

## Root cause
No technician had the skill required by the service, so availability returned no slots.

## Resolution
Added the Brakes skill to Arjun's record. Slots appeared immediately. Suggested to the product team: warn when a service has no qualified technician.
