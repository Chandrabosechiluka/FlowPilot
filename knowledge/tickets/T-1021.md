# T-1021: Everyone logged out after last night's update

## Symptoms
After the 23:00 release, every user and the FlowPilot integration got 401 and had to sign in again.

## Investigation
The release pipeline generated a new `Jwt__SigningKey` on each deployment instead of reading it from Key Vault. Tokens signed with the old key failed validation.

## Root cause
JWT signing key changed during deployment.

## Resolution
Moved the signing key to Key Vault and referenced it from the App Service settings. Users signed in again; FlowPilot re-authenticated automatically on its next call.
