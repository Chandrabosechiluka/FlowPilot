# T-1018: Login says too many attempts at opening time

## Symptoms
Every morning around 09:00 several advisors at the Gachibowli shop get 'Too many requests' when signing in.

## Investigation
Login is rate limited to 10 attempts per minute per IP. The shop's 12 staff share one office router, so they share one IP. The deployment is behind Azure App Service's front end but `Hosting:BehindProxy` was false, so in fact all shops shared the proxy's IP.

## Root cause
Rate limiting saw the proxy's address for every shop because forwarded headers were not enabled.

## Resolution
Set `Hosting__BehindProxy=true` on the App Service and restarted. Each shop now has its own limit. No code change.
