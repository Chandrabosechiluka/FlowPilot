# T-1033: Waitlist customer never got the freed slot

## Symptoms
A customer on the waitlist says a slot was freed (another customer cancelled) but they never heard anything. The offer shows as Offered with an expiry time in the past.

## Investigation
Worker logs showed `Maintenance job 'offer notifications' failed` and `'offer expiry' failed` for two hours after the database was restarted for patching; the connection pool kept failing until the API restarted.

## Root cause
Maintenance jobs failed after a database restart; offers were neither announced nor expired.

## Resolution
Restarted the API, then an Admin ran `POST /api/maintenance/run` (approved). The stale offer expired and the slot was offered to the next customer. Added a check on worker errors to the monitoring.
