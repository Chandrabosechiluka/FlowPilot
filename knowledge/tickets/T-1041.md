# T-1041: Cannot cancel an appointment: business rule violated

## Symptoms
Advisor gets 'Business rule violated' when cancelling an appointment for a customer who called at 10:15 for an 11:00 slot.

## Investigation
GarageFlow does not allow cancelling a Booked appointment less than 2 hours before it starts, unless an Admin sets `allowLate`.

## Root cause
Late-cancellation rule (2 hours notice), working as designed.

## Resolution
Explained the rule. The shop manager (Admin) cancelled it with `allowLate: true` and the reason 'customer called, emergency'.
